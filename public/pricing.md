# Pricing — Ashford Digital

All prices per month, in GBP. No setup fee. No free trial month: instead, a free private demo of your site is built before you pay.
Minimum term: 2 monthly payments, then cancel any time with 30 days' notice.

## Every plan includes
- 5-page website built for phones first
- Hosting, security and updates
- Call button that stays on screen
- Demo built before you pay
- Dashboard showing every enquiry

## Starter
- Price: £29/month
- Town pages with local content: 1

## Standard
- Price: £49/month
- Town pages with local content: 3
- Missed calls logged in your dashboard
- Photo quote form (photos + postcode become a job brief)
- Automatic review requests

## Pro
- Price: £89/month (founding price £59/month for the first 5 Pro clients)
- Town pages with local content: 10
- Everything in Standard
- Customer Engine: demand-timed local Google ads (client pays Google directly, with a spend cap), past-customer reminders, tracked referral codes, introductions to letting agents and builders
- Leads-by-source dashboard
- Guarantee: first tracked enquiry within 60 days of going live, or 3 months free. Applies when the client answers calls, supplies photos and services, the site has been live 60 days, and the Customer Engine is switched on.
